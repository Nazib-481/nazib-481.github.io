EXCEL • PERSONAL FINANCE
Bangladesh Household Case

The Rising Cost of Living

Problem: A Dhaka student/young professional earns a fixed monthly income but struggles to understand where money is going and how much can realistically be saved.

Monthly income & expense tracking Needs vs. wants analysis Budget planning & savings trend Charts and key financial insights
Insight: 12-month modeled income is ৳655K, expenses ৳463.8K and savings ৳191.2K — a 29.2% overall savings rate.

POWER BI • RETAIL
Bangladesh SME Case

Finding the Profit Behind the Sales

Problem: A small retail business sees sales every day but cannot clearly identify its strongest products, profit drivers or weak periods.

Analyse a sample retail sales dataset Compare revenue, cost & profit Product/category performance Interactive Power BI dashboard
Insight: The modeled dataset contains 600 transactions, ৳747.3K revenue and ৳112.7K gross profit; the dashboard is designed to expose product and channel profit drivers.

03
FINANCE • LISTED COMPANY
Equity Analysis Case

Inside ACI: Is the Business Getting Stronger?

Problem: An investor needs a structured view of a Bangladesh-listed company instead of relying on revenue or profit alone.

Revenue, profit & expense analysis Profitability & liquidity ratios ROA, ROE & leverage review Short analyst-style report
Insight: ACI turnover rose from ৳23,202m in FY20 to ৳46,152m in FY25, while PAT rose from ৳1,719m to ৳3,998m. FY25 PAT margin was 8.66% and ROE 13.42%.
View analysis Analysis data
Excel analysis • Ratio table • Written findings
04
BUDGET • FORECASTING
Small Business Case

Planning for Uncertainty

Problem: A small Bangladesh-based business needs to know whether it can remain profitable when sales weaken or operating costs rise.

Prepare a sample business budget Forecast revenue & expenses Best-case vs. worst-case scenarios Profit, cash need & break-even view
Insight: The modeled business earns ৳50K in the base case, ৳109.8K in the best case and loses ৳55.8K in the worst case.
