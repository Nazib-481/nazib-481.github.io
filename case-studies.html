Real-World Finance & Analytics Cases

Four practical cases built around everyday financial and business problems in Bangladesh. Each one follows the same path: problem, data, analysis, insight, recommendation. The data files are linked so the work can be checked.

Data note: the household, retail and scenario datasets are synthetic, built for portfolio use. The ACI case uses published company figures, and only the two years shown on the portfolio are filled in so far.
